    Do overseas AI tools charge GST?

    Overseas providers that sell digital services to Australian consumers are expected to add 10% GST. A GST-registered business buying for business use generally is not charged it, so check whether the checkout asks for your ABN.

    Why did my bill change when the plan didn't?

    A plan priced in US dollars is converted each time you are charged. When the Australian dollar moves, so does your bill, and some cards add an overseas transaction fee on top.

    Monthly or annual billing?

    Annual billing usually lowers the monthly rate, but you pay for the year at once and lock in that exchange rate. Run a monthly trial on real work first, then decide.

    Can AI tools help a small business?

    They can help with defined parts of a workflow, such as drafting from approved notes, exploring visual ideas or editing a recording. Start with one recurring task, and compare the useful result with the setup, checking and ongoing costs.

    Should I choose a free or paid plan?

    Start with the features and usage you need. A free plan may be enough for occasional work; a paid plan is worth it when a specific limit stops you finishing a useful recurring task.
